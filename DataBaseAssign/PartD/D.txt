Reservation Can Have at Most One Rental

- SRS: Section 7.12
- Rule: A Reservation cannot be linked to more than one Rental.
- Why Constraints Alone Are Insufficient: A Foreign Key (FK) alone does not prevent multiple Rentals from referencing the same Reservation.
- Enforcement: Apply a Unique Constraint on `RENTAL.ReservationNo`, allowing NULL values.

Vehicle Cannot Be Transferred to the Same Branch
- SRS: Section 8 — Stakeholder Notes
- Rule: FromBranchCode must be different from ToBranchCode.
- Why Constraints Alone Are Insufficient: This rule requires comparing two columns within the same row.
- Enforcement: Apply a CHECK Constraint: CHECK (FromBranchCode <> ToBranchCode).
